# Australian tax and benefits reference library

This library is the project's curated map of authoritative information used to define, implement, and validate Australian tax and benefit rules.

## Principles
- Prefer primary sources: the Australian Taxation Office, legislation and legislative instruments, Services Australia, and other responsible Australian Government agencies.
- Record the applicable financial year or effective dates for every rule.
- Distinguish enacted rules from proposals, announcements, guidance, and interpretation.
- Link to source material rather than copying long passages. Record a short summary and the specific calculation or assumption it supports.
- Re-check sources when implementing a rule and when updating support for a new financial year.
- Keep policy facts separate from implementation choices and clearly note uncertainty.

## Contents
- [Tax](tax/README.md): income tax, Medicare levy, offsets, deductions and related rules.
- [Benefits and transfers](benefits/README.md): income support, family payments, childcare support and other relevant transfers.
- [Rule inventory](rule-inventory.md): initial policy scope, modelling principles, component interactions, resource accounting, and research status.
- [Source register](source-register.md): a common record of references and review status.

The rule inventory and source register are planning aids, not evidence that any rule has been implemented or independently validated. This is an index, not legal or tax advice.
