# Benefits and transfers reference topics

Use this area to organise source notes for payments and subsidies that may affect marginal effective tax rates.

Potential topics include:
- Family Tax Benefit
- Child Care Subsidy
- Parenting Payment and other income-support payments
- Rent Assistance
- Paid Parental Leave
- Other means-tested payments or subsidies selected for the model's scope

For each topic, record the responsible agency, official source, applicable dates, income and asset tests, household and eligibility assumptions, taper or withdrawal rules, interactions with other payments, and validation examples. These are candidate topics, not a claim that the project supports them yet.
